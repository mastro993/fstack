# Direction

Follow this when the look is open (step 3 of [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md)).

**With the fstack ux-brainstorm skill installed**, hand the direction to it: invoke it (through the harness's skill tool, or by reading its `SKILL.md`) with the brief (what the product is, who uses it, the user's words about the look, any screenshot as a reference) and your step 1 map, scoped to its steps 3 and 4: gather inspiration, then let the user pick a direction from its board. Its pick, with its references, comes back as the direction; the rest of ux-brainstorm (interview, prototypes, handoff) stays out of this run.

**Without it**, do the same yourself: ask where to look (links or screenshots they have, apps they admire, an open search), group what you find into two or three named directions that disagree on a real axis (density, contrast, shape, tone), each with its references and a small HTML specimen (buttons, an input with its error, a card, the type scale, the palette), and ask the user to pick.

A screenshot is a reference: read its palette, radii, spacing rhythm, and type off it, and say which values you estimated.

With the pick question, tell the user what follows it: `DESIGN.md` written from their pick, then the hunt and a proposed system for them to confirm before anything is built.
