Great news, team! We're thrilled to announce a groundbreaking change to how we handle expenses here at Fennel Labs — one that will fundamentally transform the way we work.

It's not just a new tool. It's a whole new mindset. Not paperwork. Not delays. Just seamless reimbursement.

### ✨ Why This Matters

In today's fast-moving landscape, managing expenses efficiently is more crucial than ever. Experts agree that streamlined expense flows boost team productivity by up to 40%. Spendwise serves as a pivotal hub for all your expense needs, fostering a culture of transparency and trust.

### 🔑 Key Benefits

- **Fast:** Snap a photo of your receipt in the Spendwise app — it reads the amount for you.
- **Secure:** Your data is protected by robust, enterprise-grade security.
- **Seamless:** Reimbursement lands in 5 working days instead of the 3 weeks it takes today.

### 📅 What Happens Next

Starting 1 December, all expenses go through Spendwise at spend.fennel.example. The old expense form will remain available until 15 December, after which it will be retired — marking the end of an era!
